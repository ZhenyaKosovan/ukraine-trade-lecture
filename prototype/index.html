Read the map carefully

The gaps are part of the story.

Goods maps cover 2011 to 2023. Computer-services maps cover 2011 to 2025 and show selected partners only. Earlier trade gaps stay blank. The GDP charts start in 1991. This prototype does not show partial-year 2026 trade.

Product groups are not industries. Shaded countries show recorded trade, not the share of production made in Ukraine. Source coverage changes with occupation and war, especially from 2014 and 2022. Monetary values are nominal, not inflation-adjusted.

Sources, calculations, and reuse

Ukrstat goods statistics provide annual partner totals and two-digit UKTZED products. The product filter uses the documented teaching groups from the data package. Sector-filtered totals sum named ISO3 partners only; residual and non-HS special goods are excluded. Shares for All goods use the published world total.

NBU external-sector statistics provide computer-services trade. Shares use its published computer-services total, including partners outside the displayed list. Goods and services remain separate series.

World Bank WDI provides GDP and sector shares. The map uses GISCO 2024 boundaries, not historical borders or military control. Crimea is included in Ukraine. © EuroGeographics for the administrative boundaries. Noncommercial use only; review the geographical data terms.

Map scales are fixed across years within each series, flow, product group, and measure. Zero and missing are different. Tiny countries may not have a polygon at this map resolution. The selected-partner list still reports them. Borders do not measure statistical coverage.
